Game Rules

  1. The computer will randomly select a number between 1 and 20.
  2. You have to guess the number by entering your guess in the input field and clicking the "Guess" button.
  3. After each guess, you will receive feedback indicating whether your guess is too high, too low, or correct.
  4. You start with a score of 20, and each incorrect guess will decrease your score by 1.
  5. If you guess the correct number, your current score will be compared to the highscore, and if it's higher, it will update the highscore.
  6. You can click the "Play Again" button to reset the game and try again.
  7. Have fun and try to achieve the highest score possible!
